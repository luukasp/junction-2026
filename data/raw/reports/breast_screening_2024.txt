• 97.1% of screening results were normal, while 2.9% of participants were recalled for

• Approximately 2,500 women, or 0.9% of those screened, were referred to specialized
  healthcare services.
• A total of 1,904 cases of breast cancer or breast carcinoma in situ were detected
  through the screening programme, corresponding to about seven cases per 1,000
  screened women.
