Screening programme overview
– The national screening program for breast cancer has been operating
since 1992.
– The program’s target population consists of women aged 50–69. In
2016, full coverage in all age groups was achieved. Additionally, gender
minority individuals at risk of breast cancer are eligible for
screening. The screening method is mammography and the screening
interval is two years.
– 344 851 persons were picked from the Digital and Population Data
Services Agency database for screening invitation as of year 2024. The
invitation can be left unsent, for example due to non-disclosure for
personal safety reasons or missing address details. The Åland Islands
are excluded from the 2024 screening statistics.
– In 2024, 82 % of invitees participated in the screening program. Of
attendees, 2.9 % were invited to diagnostic examinations.
– Within the screening program, a total of 1904 breast cancer cases
were found.

Details
Data
The data includes all persons invited to screening, and their
screening related outcomes reported by spring 2026.
The data on diagnoses only includes data from the screening
registry; cancer registry data and data on diagnoses outside of the
national screening program have not been linked to obtain these figures.
For cancer detection mode classification, cancer registry data has been
utilized.
The relatively large proportion of invitation to diagnostic
examinations for the age group of 50 years, compared to other age
groups, is due to the lack of earlier mammograms for comparison.
Indicator definitions
The participation rate was calculated by dividing the number of
attendees with the number of persons invited.
Screening findings are classified by screening round, according to
the person’s most severe finding during the round.
The confidence intervals model the uncertainty related to the size
of the population and random variation. The intervals are calculated
using the exact binomial model at 95% confidence.
Population classification
Statistics Finland’s individual-level data on education were used in
the production of statistics under permit TK/2743/07.03.00/2023.
Education categories were created by combining the categories of
pre-primary, lower primary, and upper primary education into the primary
education category, combining secondary education and special vocational
qualifications into the secondary education category, and combining
lower and upper tertiary education and doctoral education into the
tertiary education category. Unknown and missing data were combined with
the primary education category. The education data is from the year
preceding the screening year (2023).
Native language and country of birth is based on data from the
Digital and Population Data Services Agency.
Result classification
Normal screening result: The next invitation will be sent in two
years, if the person still belongs to the screening target
population.
Invitation to diagnostic examinations: The person was invited to
supplementary diagnostic examinations (additional mammography,
ultrasound, biopsy).
Referral to specialized healthcare: The person was referred to
specialized care for confirmation of diagnosis and treatment.
Benign: Histologically confirmed findings classified as benign based
on SNOMED/ICD-O-3 morphology codes (behavior codes 0 or 1).
Malignant: Histologically confirmed invasive breast cancer or
carcinoma in situ (DCIS, LCIS), defined using SNOMED/ICD-O-3 morphology
codes (behavior codes 2 or 3), or cases without surgical histology but
with a TNM stage of at least I.
Other cancer: Histologically confirmed cancer of another organ
detected in breast cancer screening (based on SNOMED/ICD-O-3
coding).
No information: No histological data is available for the person
referred to specialized care.
Cancer detection mode
The cancer detection mode categories are defined as follows:
Screened cancer: After the previous screening mammography, an
invitation to diagnostic examinations was made and a biopsy was taken
within one year prior to diagnosis.
Interval cancer:
After previous screening mammography, neither an invitation to
diagnostic examinations was made or a biopsy taken within 2.5 years
prior to diagnosis.
Invitation to diagnostic examinations was made and biopsy was taken
within 2.5 years prior to diagnosis, but the date of diagnosis was 1-2.5
years after the screening mammography.
Unattended: The person was invited to screening within 2.5 years
prior to diagnosis but did not attend.
Not invited: The person was of screening age but had no registered
invitation within 2.5 years prior to diagnosis.
Younger than screening age: Diagnosis was made before the individual
reached screening age.
Older than screening age: Diagnosis was made more than 2.5 years
after exceeding the upper age limit for screening.
